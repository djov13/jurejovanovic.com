01 — Residential

House on the Hill

Graz, Austria · Residential · 2026

A compact residence shaped by its sloping site and surrounding landscape. Natural materials, generous openings, and carefully framed views create a quiet connection between interior and exterior.

02 — Competition

Markethall Tabor

Tabor, Slovenia · 2nd prize · 2024

The competition called for the redesign of all four quadrants at the intersection of two major roads. The current market premises, which were designed according to a neighborhood plan from the 1980s, no longer meet today's standards.

Together with: Miha Fujs, u.d.i.a., Lana Topolovec, m.i.a., Nebojša Drinić, m.i.a., Mihael Novak, m.i.a.

03 — Competition entry

Dejan Jovanovic is a young architect interested in the relationship between precise forms and the everyday life they hold.

He graduated in architecture in 2026 and works across residential design, competitions and architectural research.

Master thesis / 2026

Modular homes as elements of urban regeneration

The thesis asks how modular housing can repair overlooked urban fragments without erasing their character. It proposes a kit of parts that can adapt to different sites, households and stages of life.
